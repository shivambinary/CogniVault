PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY
Document ID: HR-PER-006 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Review cycle
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice each year, normally in June and December.

2. Review inputs
Reviews may consider agreed goals, quality of work, collaboration, role-related skills, reliability, and progress against development objectives. Employees should have an opportunity to provide a self-assessment.

3. Feedback meeting
Managers should discuss strengths, development areas, and goals with each employee. Feedback should be specific, respectful, and supported by examples.

4. Development plans
Where useful, the manager and employee may agree on a development plan with measurable objectives and review dates.

5. Compensation
A performance review does not automatically guarantee a salary increase, promotion, or bonus. Such decisions follow separate company processes and approvals.

6. Questions
Contact HR for questions about the review process.
