INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
INTERNSHIP POLICY
Document ID: HR-INT-015 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Internship duration
The standard internship lasts 8 to 12 weeks, unless the internship letter specifies a different period.

2. Supervision
Each intern is assigned a supervisor who provides tasks, feedback, and guidance. Interns should discuss priorities and blockers with their supervisor.

3. Working hours
Interns normally follow the team's working schedule, generally 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Any adjustment must be approved by the supervisor.

4. Remote work
Interns may work from home only when their internship supervisor explicitly approves it. The employee allowance of up to three remote days per week does not automatically apply to interns.

5. Leave and absence
Interns should notify their supervisor of planned or unexpected absence. Any leave entitlement is governed by the internship agreement and applicable HR instructions; the 24-day annual leave entitlement for full-time employees does not automatically apply.

6. Confidentiality and equipment
Interns must follow the Code of Conduct and IT Security Policy and must return company equipment at the end of the internship.

7. Completion
The supervisor may provide a completion review or certificate according to the internship agreement and program requirements.
