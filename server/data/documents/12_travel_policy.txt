INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY
Document ID: FIN-TRV-012 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Finance and Administration

1. Approval
Domestic business travel must receive manager approval before bookings are made. International travel requires approval from the department head and Finance.

2. Booking
Employees should use the company's designated travel process and select reasonably priced options that meet business needs.

3. Accommodation
Accommodation should be safe, practical, and within the budget communicated for the trip. Exceptions require written approval.

4. Claims
Employees must submit travel expense claims within 30 calendar days after completing the trip, with receipts and the business purpose.

5. Changes and cancellations
Employees should inform their manager and the travel coordinator promptly when plans change. Cancellation costs may be reviewed if the change was avoidable.

6. Personal extensions
Personal travel added to a business trip must be approved in advance. The company does not pay additional costs caused by the personal extension.
