INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document ID: FIN-EXP-011 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Finance

1. Eligible expenses
Reasonable business expenses incurred for approved company work may be reimbursed when they comply with this policy.

2. Pre-approval
Any single expense above INR 5,000 requires written manager approval before the purchase, except in a documented emergency.

3. Submission
Employees should submit claims through the expense portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction. Each claim must include an itemized receipt, business purpose, transaction date, and cost centre where applicable.

4. Review
The manager verifies business purpose and budget. Finance checks documentation and policy compliance before payment.

5. Non-reimbursable items
Personal purchases, traffic fines, unsupported claims, and expenses unrelated to approved business activities are not reimbursable.

6. Payment timing
Approved claims are normally processed in the next scheduled reimbursement cycle. Processing may take up to 15 business days after Finance approval.

7. Questions
Contact finance@intellicorp.example.
