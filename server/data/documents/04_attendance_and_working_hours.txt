ATTENDANCE AND WORKING HOURS POLICY
Document ID: HR-ATT-004 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Standard hours
The standard schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including a one-hour lunch break.

2. Recording attendance
Employees must record their start time, end time, and approved absences in the designated attendance system. Employees should not record attendance on behalf of another person.

3. Late arrival
Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their manager as soon as possible. Repeated lateness may be reviewed by the manager and HR.

4. Flexible schedules
A flexible schedule may be approved by a manager when the role and team requirements allow it. Approval should be documented.

5. Corrections
If an attendance entry is incorrect, the employee should submit a correction request through the attendance system within five working days.

6. Questions
Contact HR for attendance-record questions and the manager for team scheduling questions.
