INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Document ID: GEN-FAQ-016 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: General

Q1. What are the standard working hours?
The standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour lunch break.

Q2. How many annual leave days do full-time employees receive?
Eligible full-time employees receive 24 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Interns and contractors should check their own agreements and policies.

Q3. How many days can eligible employees work from home?
Eligible full-time employees may work from home up to three days per week with manager approval. Interns require explicit supervisor approval.

Q4. Who should I contact about IT problems?
Contact it-support@intellicorp.example. For suspected security incidents or phishing, contact security@intellicorp.example.

Q5. What is the approval threshold for an individual expense?
A single expense above INR 5,000 requires written manager approval before purchase, except in a documented emergency.

Q6. When should I submit an expense claim?
Submit expense claims within 30 calendar days of the transaction, with the required receipts and business purpose.

Q7. How often are formal performance reviews held?
Formal performance reviews are normally held twice a year, in June and December.

Q8. How long is the standard internship?
The standard internship lasts 8 to 12 weeks unless the internship letter states otherwise.
