INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
DATA PRIVACY AND INFORMATION HANDLING POLICY
Document ID: IT-DAT-017 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Information Technology

1. Data classification
Company information should be handled according to its sensitivity: Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. The data owner or manager should clarify classification when uncertain.

2. Minimum necessary access
Employees should access and share only the information needed for their work. Confidential and Restricted information must only be shared with authorized recipients.

3. Approved storage
Company work files must be stored in approved company-managed systems. Personal email accounts, personal cloud drives, and unapproved file-sharing services must not be used for company information.

4. External tools and AI services
Employees must not submit Confidential or Restricted company information to public AI tools or external services unless the service has been reviewed and explicitly approved by IT and Security.

5. Retention and deletion
Records must be retained and deleted according to the applicable retention schedule. Employees should not independently destroy records subject to a legal, audit, or investigation hold.

6. Suspected exposure
Report accidental disclosure, misdirected email, lost storage media, or suspected data exposure to security@intellicorp.example immediately.
