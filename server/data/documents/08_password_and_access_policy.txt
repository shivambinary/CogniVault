INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
PASSWORD AND ACCESS MANAGEMENT POLICY
Document ID: IT-ACC-008 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Information Technology

1. Individual access
Every user must use their own account. Shared user accounts are prohibited unless IT has documented an approved technical exception.

2. Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and should use a passphrase or a combination of character types. Passwords must not be reused across company and personal services.

3. Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for email, remote access, and systems that support it.

4. Access requests
Managers must approve access requests for their team members. IT grants access according to job responsibilities and the principle of least privilege.

5. Access removal
Managers must notify IT when an employee changes roles or leaves the organization so that access can be reviewed or disabled.

6. Suspected compromise
Users must report suspected credential exposure to IT immediately and follow the account recovery instructions.
