INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
LAPTOP AND DEVICE USAGE POLICY
Document ID: IT-DEV-009 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Information Technology

1. Company equipment
Company-issued laptops and devices are provided primarily for business activities. Employees are responsible for reasonable care of assigned equipment.

2. Physical security
Devices should not be left unattended in public places. Employees should use a screen lock and keep devices physically secure while travelling.

3. Updates and protection
Employees must not disable endpoint protection or postpone critical security updates without IT approval.

4. Personal use
Limited personal use is permitted if it does not interfere with work, violate company policy, or create security or legal risks.

5. Loss or damage
Lost, stolen, or damaged equipment must be reported to IT as soon as possible.

6. Return of equipment
Employees must return company equipment when requested and as part of the exit process.
