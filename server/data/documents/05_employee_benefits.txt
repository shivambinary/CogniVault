INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE BENEFITS OVERVIEW
Document ID: HR-BEN-005 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Eligibility
Benefits depend on employment category, location, and the employee's signed offer or benefits confirmation. This overview is a summary and does not replace individual benefit documents.

2. Health coverage
Eligible full-time employees may enroll in the company health-insurance plan during onboarding or an announced enrollment window. Coverage details, exclusions, dependants, and claim procedures are described in the insurer's plan documents.

3. Learning support
Employees may request reimbursement for approved job-related courses or certifications. Prior manager approval and proof of payment are required. The annual learning-support limit is INR 15,000 per eligible employee.

4. Wellness
The company periodically shares information about wellness sessions and employee-assistance resources through the HR portal.

5. Questions
Contact benefits@intellicorp.example for eligibility and enrollment questions.
