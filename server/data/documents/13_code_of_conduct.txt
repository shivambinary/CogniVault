INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
CODE OF CONDUCT
Document ID: GOV-CON-013 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Respectful workplace
Employees must treat others fairly and respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threats, and retaliation are not acceptable.

2. Conflicts of interest
Employees should disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to their manager or HR. Employees must not use their position for improper personal benefit.

3. Confidentiality
Employees must protect non-public company, customer, employee, and partner information. Confidential information should only be shared with authorized people for legitimate business purposes.

4. Gifts and hospitality
Employees must not accept or offer gifts that could improperly influence a business decision. Uncertain cases should be disclosed to the manager or Compliance.

5. Reporting concerns
Concerns may be raised with HR or the designated compliance contact. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practicable, consistent with a fair review.

6. Non-retaliation
Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
