INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Document ID: HR-GEN-001 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Purpose
This handbook introduces employees to the workplace practices and general expectations of IntelliCorp Technologies. It is a demonstration document created for the IntelliCorp RAG academic project.

2. Working hours
The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour lunch break. Some teams may follow approved schedules based on operational requirements. Employees should coordinate schedule changes with their manager.

3. Professional conduct
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners respectfully; communicate honestly; protect company information; and follow applicable workplace policies.

4. Attendance
Employees should record attendance using the approved attendance system. If an employee expects to be late or absent, they should inform their manager as early as possible and follow the leave or attendance process.

5. Questions and support
Questions about employment policies should be directed to the Human Resources team at hr@intellicorp.example. IT issues should be directed to it-support@intellicorp.example.

6. Policy precedence
This handbook is a general guide. Where a specific policy provides more detailed instructions, the specific policy should be consulted. If two documents appear inconsistent, employees should contact HR rather than assume which rule applies.
