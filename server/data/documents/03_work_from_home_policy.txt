INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
REMOTE WORK / WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Document ID: HR-REM-003 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Standard arrangement
Eligible full-time employees may work from home for up to three days per week, subject to manager approval and team requirements.

2. Request and approval
Employees should agree their remote-work days with their manager in advance. A calendar entry or written approval should record the agreed arrangement. Approval may be changed when business operations require in-person attendance.

3. Availability
Employees working remotely must remain reachable during their agreed working hours, attend scheduled meetings, and keep their calendar up to date.

4. Equipment and connectivity
Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection and a work environment suitable for handling company information. Company-issued equipment must be used in accordance with the IT Security Policy.

5. Confidentiality
Employees must prevent unauthorized people from viewing company information. Sensitive work should not be discussed in public places where it may be overheard.

6. Interns
Interns may work remotely only when their internship supervisor has explicitly approved the arrangement. The standard three-day allowance for eligible full-time employees does not automatically apply to interns.

7. Contact
Questions should be directed to the employee's manager or HR.
