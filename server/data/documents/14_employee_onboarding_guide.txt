INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
Document ID: HR-ONB-014 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Before the first day
HR sends the offer documents, joining instructions, and a list of information required to complete employment records.

2. First day
New employees meet HR, receive their access credentials and assigned equipment where applicable, and review the Employee Handbook and IT Security Policy.

3. First week
The manager explains role expectations, team processes, communication channels, and initial goals. New employees should complete mandatory security and conduct training.

4. System access
Access to internal systems is requested by the manager and provisioned by IT according to role requirements.

5. Questions
Contact hr@intellicorp.example for onboarding questions and it-support@intellicorp.example for account or device issues.
