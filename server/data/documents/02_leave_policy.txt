INTELLICORP TECHNOLOGIES
LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: HR-LEV-002 | Version: 1.0 | Effective date: 01 January 2026
Department: Human Resources

1. Annual leave entitlement
Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Leave entitlement for employees who join during the year is calculated proportionally according to their joining date and employment terms.

2. Applying for planned leave
Employees should submit planned leave requests through the HR portal at least five working days before the first day of leave. The manager reviews requests based on team coverage and business needs. A request is not approved until the employee receives confirmation in the HR portal or from the manager.

3. Sick leave
Employees who are unable to work because of illness should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and record the absence in the HR portal. HR may request supporting documentation where permitted by applicable rules and company procedures.

4. Emergency leave
For an emergency, employees should notify their manager as soon as practical. The employee should complete the HR portal record when they are able to do so.

5. Carry-forward
A maximum of five unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March. Any exception requires written HR approval.

6. Interns and contractors
Intern leave entitlements are defined in the Internship Policy or the relevant internship agreement. Contractors should refer to their contract. The 24-day annual entitlement in this policy applies to eligible full-time employees.

7. Contact
For questions about leave balances or eligibility, contact hr@intellicorp.example.
